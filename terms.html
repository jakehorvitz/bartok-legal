Bartok Notifications: SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Program description. Bartok Notifications sends batch approval requests, one daily digest, and order or refund status notices from Bartok, the registered sole-proprietor brand of Jake Horvitz, to Jake Horvitz's own mobile number. The program is single-recipient: it has no customers, subscribers, or third-party recipients, and it sends no marketing or promotional content and no third-party content of any kind.

How to opt in. Text the keyword START to +1 805-866-0420 from your own mobile phone. You will receive a confirmation message ("Bartok Notifications: you are opted in. Msg freq varies, msg and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel."). Nothing is sent until that START has been received and recorded. There is no web form, checkbox, or purchase involved, and nothing is ever pre-selected. See the opt-in page for the full call to action.

Message frequency. Varies by account activity; up to 10 messages per day.

Message and data rates may apply. Charges are billed by your mobile carrier.

Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Opt-out. Reply STOP at any time to cancel. After you send STOP, you will receive no further messages unless you opt in again by texting START.

Help. Reply HELP for help, or contact jakeharrisonhorvitz@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
