Bartok Notifications — SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective date: August 31, 2026

Program description. Bartok Notifications sends account notifications, daily digests, and approval requests from Jake Horvitz, a sole proprietor, to the operator's own mobile number. The program is single-recipient: it has no customers, subscribers, or third-party recipients, and it sends no marketing or promotional content and no third-party content of any kind.

How to opt in. A number is enrolled only by actively typing YES at the consent screen shown during setup of the software. Nothing is pre-checked or pre-filled. See the opt-in workflow and consent proof.

Message frequency. Varies by account activity; typically up to 10 messages per day.

Message and data rates may apply. Charges are billed by your mobile carrier.

Opt-out. Reply STOP at any time to cancel. After you send STOP, you will receive no further messages unless you opt in again.

Help. Reply HELP for help, or contact jakeharrisonhorvitz@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
