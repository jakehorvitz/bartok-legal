Bartok Notifications

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Bartok Notifications is a personal, single-recipient SMS notification program sent by Bartok, the registered sole-proprietor brand of Jake Horvitz, Santa Barbara, California, to Jake Horvitz himself at his own mobile number.

What the program does

Bartok is self-hosted software that assists him with his own business administration. When it needs a decision from him, or has recorded an action on one of his own accounts, it sends a text message to his own mobile phone so he can approve, decline, or act on it while away from his desk. It sends exactly three kinds of message:

  • Batch approval requests: how many items are waiting and the reply code to approve or decline them.
  • One daily digest: a once-daily summary of items that changed or expired, which also carries the opt-out instruction.
  • Order or refund status notices on his own store account, telling him an action was recorded for him to execute himself.

Who receives these messages. Only one person: the operator himself, at his own mobile number. The software has no recipient parameter, so his configured number is the only destination a message can be sent to. The program has no customers, subscribers, or third-party recipients, and it sends no marketing, promotional, or third-party content of any kind.
